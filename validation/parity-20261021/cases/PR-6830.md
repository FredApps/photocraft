## Pinned upstream reproduction and regression specification

Part of #6760: the Full Screen Mode part only, as asked in the review. Tab outside Full Screen is unchanged: `hidden_by_tab` and "Tab restores only what it hid" stay as on main.

- **Tab in Full Screen Mode** shows the menu bar, the options bar, the toolbar and the dock over the full-screen canvas. Tab again hides them. The status bar stays hidden, as before.
- Tab in Full Screen only flips a new non-saved flag (`Panels::full_screen_chrome`, `#[serde(skip)]`). It does not touch the toolbar, options bar, dock or `hidden_by_tab` settings.
- **Leaving Full Screen Mode** clears the flag, so the panels come back exactly as they were before entering, whatever number of Tabs was pressed. This fixes the case from the review (two Tabs, then leaving, left every panel hidden).

Test: `tab_in_full_screen_shows_and_hides_the_chrome_and_leaving_restores_the_panels` runs from three starting states (default, options bar hidden by hand, everything hidden by Tab). In each, Tab once shows the chrome, Tab twice hides it, the panel settings never change, and leaving after one or two Tabs restores them exactly. Main's `tab_hides_all_panels_and_shift_tab_only_the_dock` is unchanged.
