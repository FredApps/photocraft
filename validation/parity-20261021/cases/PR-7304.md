## Summary
Holding Shift while dragging with the Freeform Pen keeps the stroke on a straight line at 0°, 45°, or 90°, reusing the same constraint logic as painting tools. Addresses the path-drawing modifier gap in #7171 (the standard Pen tool already constrains ⇧-clicks to 45° rays).
