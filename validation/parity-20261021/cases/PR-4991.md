Addresses #4923.

A click on the pasteboard previously left the existing layer selection unchanged, even when Move Auto-Select found no layer under the pointer.

Update the Move tool's empty-area marquee handling so an ordinary click outside the document deselects all layers.

Preserve the existing interactions:
- Clicking transparent pixels inside the document does not deselect layers.
- Shift-clicking the pasteboard preserves the current selection.
- Dragging from the pasteboard still creates a layer-selection marquee.
