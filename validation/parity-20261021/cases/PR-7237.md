Fixes #7183

**Problem.** In Camera Raw's post-crop vignette, Midpoint 0 put the falloff circle exactly on the frame edge, so the whole border darkened. At the lowest Midpoint the circle should be larger than the frame, so only the very corners get darker.

**Change.** `crates/algo/src/camera_raw.rs`, `vignette()`: the inner radius mapping goes from `1.0 - mid * 0.8` to `1.4 - mid * 1.2`. Midpoint 0 now places the circle beyond the frame (corners only), and Midpoint 100 stays tight to the centre. Note that this also moves the falloff for intermediate Midpoint values, including the default.

**Test.** `vignette_midpoint_zero_targets_corners_not_frame_edges`: on a 200×100 flat grey frame with Amount −80, Midpoint 0, Feather 50, the corner is darker than the middle of the top edge and the centre stays neutral.
