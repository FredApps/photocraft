## Root cause

`polygon_click` (Polygonal Lasso) closed the outline whenever a click with at least three vertices placed landed within 8 screen points of the first vertex. When you draw a small rectangle, the fourth corner sits one side length from the first. If that side is shorter than 8 points, the click closed the polygon on the third vertex and the selection became a triangle. The Lasso's Alt-click polygonal mode (`lasso_ui.rs`) had the same fixed 8-point reach.

## Fix

Both tools now take their close reach from a new shared helper, `canvas::polygon_close_reach`. The reach is still 8 points on screen, but it is never more than a quarter of the outline's own on-screen size (and never less than 1 point). Outlines larger than 32 points keep exactly today's behaviour. On small shapes, the last corner adds a vertex, and a click on the first vertex (or a double-click) still closes the outline. Non-finite points are ignored.

## Tests

- `polygon_lasso_tests::the_last_corner_of_a_small_polygon_does_not_close_it` clicks the four corners of a 6x6 square at 100% zoom. It checks that the polygon stays open with 4 vertices, then that a click on the first vertex closes it as a square. The test fails without the fix ("four corners, still open").
- `polygon_lasso_tests::polygon_close_reach_scales_with_small_outlines_only` checks that large outlines keep the full 8-point reach at zoom 1 and 2, that small outlines get a smaller reach with a 1-point floor, and that NaN input falls back to 8.
