Keep shape fill and stroke alpha through a PSD round-trip.

Fixes #6180.

Photoshop colour descriptors (`RGBC` and friends) have no alpha channel, so a rectangle fill at 50% opacity saved as opaque. PhotoCraft now writes a private `pcAl` (0..1) key on the colour descriptor for fill, which Photoshop ignores. Stroke colour alpha is folded into `strokeStyleOpacity` because that field is the PSD-native stroke opacity.

Tests: `color_descriptor_keeps_photocraft_alpha`, `vstk_folds_color_alpha_into_stroke_opacity`.
