## Pinned upstream reproduction and regression specification

Closes #5956.

While typing, any press outside the text committed it (`type_tool::pointer_down`: "a click outside commits"). Photoshop 2025 moves the text when the press is just outside it, shows the Move cursor there, and keeps typing. Only a press further out commits.

### Photoshop, measured

On macOS at 100 %, while typing point text:

| Where the drag starts | Photoshop |
|---|---|
| about 20 pt below and left of the glyphs | moves the text, still typing |
| 35 pt to the right of the glyphs | moves the text, still typing |
| 48 pt from the glyphs | commits the text, which doesn't move |
| about 60 pt or 100 pt away | commits the text, which doesn't move |

### Change

- **`type_transform`:** `pointer` gets a plain-press branch. When the press is outside the text frame but within `MOVE_REACH_PT` (40 screen points) of it, it starts the existing temporary type transform with `Hit::Inside`, which is a move.
  - That's the same gesture the ⌘/Ctrl drag inside the frame already uses. Typing carries on, and the release applies one `type.edit` transform in the session.
  - The start code that the ⌘ branch and the new branch share moved into `start`.
- **Unchanged:** a paragraph box's handles still resize the box. Further out, a press commits as before.
- **`cursor`:** shows Move in that zone (`move_zone`), so the pointer tells you a drag will move the text.

### Tests

- New: `a_drag_just_outside_the_typed_text_moves_it` (type_tool_tests). It checks:
  - 20 pt right of the frame the cursor is Move, and a drag moves the text by the drag, keeps typing, and adds one History step;
  - 100 pt away there is no Move cursor, and a click commits.

  It fails without the fix: no Move cursor, and the press commits.
- `cargo fmt`, `clippy -D warnings`, the ui-egui suite (3,193 tests), `cargo xtask wasm` and `cargo xtask layers` pass. I ran these in this branch's own target directory.

### Verified in the app

On macOS 27 with a release build, side by side with Photoshop 2025:

- **Near the text:** I typed "Hello" with the Type tool and dragged from about 25 pt to the right of it. The text moved with the drag and the caret stayed, so typing carries on.
- **Far from it:** a click about 90 pt away committed the text where it had been moved.

Both match Photoshop.
