## Problem
CMYK JPEG exports included a JFIF APP0 marker alongside a four-component frame and Adobe APP14. Windows Photos rejected the resulting file, matching the CMYK/JFIF issue confirmed in VectorCraft #3084.

## Fix
Remove only JFIF APP0 from CMYK output while retaining Adobe APP14. Preserve available DPI in EXIF, including when the image has no source EXIF or its valid EXIF block has no resolution tags. RGB and gray JPEG density behavior is unchanged.
