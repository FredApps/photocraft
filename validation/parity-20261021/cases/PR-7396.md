Refs storytold/PhotoCraft#5745. Split out of #5816 per review.

**Cause:** panorama Auto-Blend called `seam_blend` with gain compensation always off, so Seamless Tones and Colors only feathered the seam. An exposure difference between the photos stayed visible on each side.

**Fix:** pass `seamless` for the gain-compensation flag too, the way Photomerge evens out tone and colour over the overlaps.

**Test:** `auto_blend_panorama_seamless_matches_exposure`: two layers with a 10% exposure step. The merged layer's blue reads within 0.015 on both sides (0.5 vs 0.55 before).
