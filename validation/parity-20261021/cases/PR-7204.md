Fixes #6995

### Problem
A layer effect set to **Dissolve** painted exactly like Normal. In Photoshop, a Dissolve Outer Glow turns into scattered, noisy speckle that thins out with the glow. Ours stayed a smooth fade. The effect painters passed Dissolve to `psblend::composite`, which has no noise and so blends it as Normal. Only a Dissolve *layer* got the per-pixel choice (`blend_into`, `fs_blend`). The open PRs on Dissolve (#3193, #4711) cover a Dissolve layer with effects, adjustments and clipping, not an effect's own mode.

### Solution
An effect in Dissolve mode now covers each pixel fully or not at all. The chance is its coverage × opacity, and the pixel is picked with the same document-coordinate noise as a Dissolve layer.

- **CPU** (`crates/compose/src/effects.rs`):
  - `paint` handles Dissolve. Every effect drawn through it gets it: drop and inner shadows, outer and inner glows (including gradient glows), colour, gradient and pattern overlays, satin, inside and centre strokes, and bevel highlights and shadows.
  - Outside strokes build their own coverage share. A Dissolve stroke's share becomes 0 or 1 the same way.
- **GPU** (`crates/gpu/src/compose.wgsl`): the same rule. `fx_composite` serves `fs_fxpaint` and the vector-stroke step in `fs_fxinit`, and `fs_fxstroke` resolves an outside stroke's share. An outside stroke runs two passes, its colour and its coverage, and `crates/gpu/src/plan.rs` now gives the coverage pass the stroke's mode as well; it used to be Normal there. Before that, the colour was all or nothing while the coverage stayed smooth, so the result was half transparent.
- **Tiling:** `maps_are_local` now treats a Dissolve effect like noise. Dissolve turns any coverage above zero into a chance of a full pixel. A tile's maps could carry blur rounding that differs from the whole layer's, so a pixel could flip at a tile edge.

### Tests
- `compose::tests::dissolve_effects_are_speckled_not_faded`. It fails before this change.
  - A Dissolve outer glow: every pixel in the ring is either the glow colour or the backdrop, with more speckle near the shape than further out. The Normal glow fades smoothly.
  - A tile renders exactly the same pixels as the whole document.
  - A 50 % Dissolve outside stroke: about half of the band is covered, and no pixel is half blue.
- `gpu/tests/parity.rs` `effect_cases` gains a Dissolve outer glow, inner gradient glow, drop shadow and outside stroke, compared with the CPU at U8 and U16.
