Fixes #6015

## Root cause
`marquee_corners` (`crates/ui-egui/src/canvas.rs`) built a fixed-size marquee from the press point plus the size, and `marquee_px` then floors the lower corner and ceils the upper one. The press lands somewhere inside a pixel, so `start + size` spans a pixel more each way: 1 × 1 selected 2 × 2, 10 × 10 selected 11 × 11, as reported. (A normal drag is unaffected: both its corners are pointer positions.)

## Fix
With Style: Fixed Size the corners land on the pixel grid: from the pressed pixel towards the drag (dragging up or left takes the pixels before it), or, with ⌥ during the drag, centred on the press, as before. `marquee_px`'s floor and ceil then add nothing, so the selection is exactly the size the options bar shows.

## Tests
`canvas::marquee_size_tests::a_fixed_size_marquee_is_exactly_that_size`: 1 × 1, 10 × 10 and 64 × 32 from a press at (10.4, 20.6), dragged right/down, on the press itself, up/left, and ⌥-centred: the selected rectangle is exactly the size each time (on `main` each case is a pixel larger both ways). The existing marquee tests (`cargo test -p photocraft-ui-egui marquee`, 30 tests, incl. `alt_draws_from_the_centre_and_shift_alt_a_centred_circle` and `shift_square_pressed_between_pixels_has_equal_sides`) still pass; `cargo clippy -p photocraft-ui-egui --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo fmt` clean, on a Linux (WSL) build host.
