**TL;DR:** the dock column can collapse to a strip of panel icons and expand again, like Photoshop's double arrow.

Fixes #4059.

Photoshop's dock column has a double arrow on top that collapses the panels to a strip of icons and expands them again. PhotoCraft had no equivalent, so the dock always took its full width.

## Change
- A bar on top of the dock column holds the double arrow (Collapse to Icons / Expand Panels). Double-clicking the bar, its context menu and every panel menu toggle it too.
- Collapsed, each visible panel shows as an icon (the tooltip is the panel name), with a line between groups. Clicking an icon opens its group as a flyout beside the strip on that panel. Clicking it again closes it, and so does a click outside when Preferences › Workspace › Auto-Collapse Iconic Panels is on.
- Window › <panel> and the icon rail open the panel in its flyout. Choosing it again while it shows hides it, as in the expanded dock. Right-to-left layouts mirror the strip and the flyout.
- The state is `DockLayout::iconic` (`ui.set {"dock": {"iconic": true}}`), saved with workspaces and the remembered layout. Older layouts load unchanged. The open flyout is not saved.
- The new strings are translated in every catalog.
