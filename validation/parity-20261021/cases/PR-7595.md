Closes #7316.

In a window too short for the tools, the toolbar's tool column scrolls. egui then widens the UI by the scroll bar's width, and the colour chips, which were centred in `ui.available_width()`, moved right with it. Default Colors and Switch Colors moved with them. The shift is about 3 px in the Studio and Pro themes, and more with Classic's wider solid bar. That matches the screenshots in the issue: the lighter strip at the right of the tool column is the scroll bar.

### Fix

`color_chips` is now centred in the tool column's width as it was before the tools' `ScrollArea` (`full.width()`, captured above it). The chips therefore sit under the tools whether or not the column scrolls. The Pro themes had the same shift in a short enough window; the reporter's window was just tall enough to hide it.

### Tests

- `color_chips_stay_centred_when_the_tools_scroll`: for every theme, with one and two columns, at 1x and 2x, the chips paint at the same x in a 420 pt window (the tools scroll) as in a 1400 pt one. It runs a few frames, since the scroll bar only appears once the overflow has been measured. It fails without the fix (`[18.0, 28.0]` vs `[15.0, 25.0]`).
- `cargo fmt`, `clippy -D warnings`, the ui-egui suite, `cargo xtask wasm` and `cargo xtask layers` pass.

### Verified in the app

On macOS 27, Studio theme, in a 552 pt tall window where the tools scroll, measured against the tool buttons' centre of 25.0 pt:

- **Before:** white chip and swap arrows centred at 27.75 pt.
- **After:** 24.5 pt and 24.75 pt.

Classic looks right too. For reference, Photoshop 2025 centres its swatches under the tool icons in the same way.
