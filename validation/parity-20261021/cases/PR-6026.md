Fixes #6015.

**Problem:** with the Rectangular (or Elliptical) Marquee set to Style › Fixed Size, the selection was one pixel larger on each side than the size entered: 10 × 10 selected 11 × 11, and 1 × 1 selected 2 × 2. The fixed rectangle starts at the press point, which usually sits between pixel edges (e.g. x = 50.4, so 50.4..60.4). `marquee_px` then floors the near edge and ceils the far edge, which gives 50..61. This is the same cause as #5472 for ⇧ squares.

**Fix:** `canvas::fixed_size_px` builds the Fixed Size rectangle on whole pixels: the near edge goes to the closest pixel edge and the far edge is exactly W × H away. Both the live outline and readout and the selection made on release use it, through `marquee_preview_px`. A click without a drag now places the Fixed Size selection, as in Photoshop. Before, a click deselected. Normal and Fixed Ratio are unchanged: a click still deselects.

**Tests:**
- `canvas::tests::fixed_size_px_is_exactly_the_size`: down-right and up-left, 1 × 1, fractional sizes.
- `marquee_tests::fixed_size_selects_exactly_the_entered_size`: through the real canvas, for both marquee tools and 10 × 10, 1 × 1 and 64 × 32. The press lands between pixels, then the test checks the readout, the dragged selection and a click.

**Checks run locally:** the marquee tests in `photocraft-ui-egui` (`fixed_size`, `marquee_styles`, `square_px`, `one_pixel`, `the_live_outline`), `cargo clippy -p photocraft-ui-egui --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo fmt --check`. No new UI strings. CI covers the rest.
