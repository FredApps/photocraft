Fixes #7844



### Problem

Layer › Smart Objects › Replace Contents with contents of another pixel size kept the placement's scale but pinned the new contents to the old placement's top-left corner. Photoshop keeps the placement's centre (the transform's reference point), so new contents grow or shrink around the middle. Mockup templates rely on that: a print area whose artwork changes size stays where the designer centred it.



In the issue's case, a 200 × 200 placement centred at (500, 500) and replaced by 400 × 200 contents ended up centred at (600, 500), not (500, 500). At 150 % it ended up at (650, 500).



### Solution

`crates/engine/src/smart_cmds.rs`:

- **`replace_contents_bytes`** compares the old and new contents' bounds before the swap. It uses `source_image`, the cached decode the re-render uses anyway, so nothing is decoded twice. Each instance's placement is then moved by the difference of their centres, in source pixels. The new contents' centre lands where the old one did, and the placement's scale, rotation and skew are kept.

- **`move_in_source`** composes that move under the placement: `transform · translate(dx, dy)`. For a perspective placement it adds the move to the homography's third column (`H · T`).

- **What doesn't move:**

  - A warped or stacked instance is left as it was. Its mesh or stack is laid out over the old contents' box, so moving the placement wouldn't move the warp with it.

  - When the old contents can't be decoded (an unavailable linked file), the placement is kept as before.

  - Contents of the same size don't move.

- **Undo:** still one step for all instances.



Relink and Update Modified Content are unchanged.



**#6914** (Replace Contents `fit`: stretch / contain) touches the same command. Its default `keep` is this behaviour, so the two fit together: with this change, `keep` keeps the centre as Photoshop does.



### Tests

- **New `smart_cmds::tests::replaced_contents_of_another_size_keep_the_placement_centre`**, the issue's case scaled down: a 20 × 20 placement centred at (50, 50) replaced by 40 × 20 contents.

  - At 100 % the bounds go from (40, 40, 60, 60) to (30, 40, 70, 60).

  - At 150 % they go from (35, 35, 65, 65) to (19, 34, 81, 66): still centred on (50, 50). Resampling softens one edge pixel on each side, as the issue's expected width of 601 for 600 also shows.

  - Replacing with the same contents again doesn't move them.



  It fails without the fix: the bounds stay pinned to the top-left.

- **Updated `export_replace_and_linked_contents`:** it asserted the old top-left anchoring. It now checks that the replaced contents keep the old centre (within a pixel) and their scale, and it samples the linked update inside the new placement.
