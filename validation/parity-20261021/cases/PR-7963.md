Found while testing `crates/engine/src/file_cmds.rs:768` (no existing issue): File Info shows an exposure just under one second as "1/1 s".

## Cause
`exposure_text` used the `1/n s` form whenever the exposure was under 1 s and its reciprocal was within 1% of a whole number. For exposures from about 0.99 s to just under 1 s, the reciprocal rounds to 1, so it printed "1/1 s".

## Fix
The `1/n s` form is now used only when the rounded reciprocal is greater than 1. Exposures near one second fall through to the decimal form, so 0.996 s reads "1 s". A regression test covers 0.996, 0.99 and 1/250.

## Verified
- Regression test `exposure_text_near_one_second_is_not_one_over_one`: fails on `main` without the fix and passes with it (`cargo test -p photocraft-engine --lib exposure_text_near_one_second_is_not_one_over_one`).
- `cargo xtask ci` passes.

## Not changed
The decimal formatting and the 1% tolerance for other exposures are unchanged.
