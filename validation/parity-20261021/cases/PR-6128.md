## What

Closes #6107.

The Brush Preset picker's preset cards started at the standard footer scale (1.0), so the grid showed only a few large thumbnails and users had to drag the footer slider smaller on every fresh profile. The picker now starts at the compact scale (0.7, the threshold at which tips drop their size numbers and fill their cell), so more brushes fit by default.

- `brush_picker::DEFAULT_SCALE` (0.7) is used by `list_state()`, which is also the serde default for `UiState::brush_picker_list`.
- Nothing else changes: the footer slider, the gear's card parts, and the scale remembered across restarts (`Preferences::brush_picker`) still win over the default, so existing profiles are untouched.
- The Brushes tab (`brushes_panel`) keeps its standard-size cards.
