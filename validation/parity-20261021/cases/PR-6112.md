Fixes #5638.



Adding Noise to a shadow or glow can paint speckles across otherwise empty parts of the layer's bounds. The running-sum blur leaves tiny positive cancellation residues after its last covered sample; the noise stage amplifies those residues into visible coverage. On main df055f3d, a synthetic sloping shape with a 5 px blur and 26% noise produces 280 visible pixels where the direct convolution is exactly empty.



Track the last covered sample and reset the sum only when its window is empty. This preserves real faint coverage without an opacity cutoff. Tests compare the noisy blur against the existing direct-convolution support at integral/fractional widths and check empty gaps plus 1e-20 coverage. No UI, dependency, or workflow changes are included.
