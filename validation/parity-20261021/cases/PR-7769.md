Addresses #7629 for the Lasso's ⌥ mode. Measured; the reasoning for the dedicated Polygonal Lasso is at the end, and if the report is about that one instead, say so and I'll keep looking.

### What I measured

With the **Lasso** tool and ⌥ held (Photoshop's straight-segment mode), a click closer to the last vertex than the radius for grabbing the first one ended the selection instead of placing the vertex:

```
three ⌥ clicks at (50,50) (90,50) (90,90)   →  drag points = 3
a fourth ⌥ click at (85,91), 5 points away  →  before: drag = None, selection committed (50,50)-(90,90)
                                               after:  drag points = 4, nothing committed
```

That radius is `8.0 / point_zoom`, so every segment shorter than 8 points on screen was impossible to draw: the click meant to continue the outline finished it instead, and tracing anything small meant the selection kept ending after three vertices.

### What changed

Both closing gestures stay; they just stop sharing one radius:

- **clicking the first vertex** is aiming at a target, and keeps the 8-point grab radius;
- **clicking the last vertex again** is the same click repeated, so it only has to allow for a hand's jitter — 3 points (`SAME_POINT`).

Everything else is untouched: Enter closes, Escape cancels, Backspace and right-click retract, releasing ⌥ with the button up closes, and the Freeform Pen still ends an open path by clicking its last point.

### Tests

`a_short_alt_lasso_segment_places_a_vertex_instead_of_closing` pins all three cases: the 5-point click places a fourth vertex with nothing committed; a click 1–2 points from the last vertex still closes the outline; and a click 4–5 points from the *first* vertex still closes it. On `main` the first case leaves `drag = None` with the triangle already committed.

The existing `clicking_the_last_point_again_closes_the_outline` passes unchanged — it clicks 1.4 points from the last vertex, inside the jitter allowance. My first attempt dropped that gesture for the Lasso altogether and the gate caught it, which is what sent me to the two-radius version.

### The dedicated Polygonal Lasso, for the record

I could not reproduce it there, and I checked the two mechanisms that looked likely:

- `polygon_click` closes only near the **first** vertex, so nothing stops a short segment;
- egui reports a double click from the time between clicks **and** their distance (`max_click_dist`, 6 points), so two quick clicks further apart than that are not a double click. A probe placing a fourth vertex 4 units from the third, clicked in the next frame, keeps the vertex and commits nothing.

So if the reporter was on the Polygonal Lasso proper, this is not their bug, and their zoom level and roughly how short the segments were would tell us where to look.
