Fixes #2091 (the remaining part).

`widgets::menu_scroll` already caps right-click menus (the layer's, the canvas tools', …) at the visible window height and scrolls them. As the reporter pointed out, the only cue was egui's thin floating scroll bar, which is invisible until hovered, so nothing showed that more items were hidden.

**Change.** When a menu overflows (known from the previous frame), `menu_scroll` reserves a 14 pt row above and below the items and draws a ▲ / ▼ arrow in each, painted as triangles so it doesn't depend on font glyphs:
- hovering an arrow scrolls that way, at 400 pt/s with repaints while hovered, like Photoshop's and the system menus;
- clicking an arrow jumps 200 pt;
- an arrow dims at its end of the list.

Menus that fit are unchanged. All 10 callers get this through the shared helper.

**Test.** `overflowing_menus_get_scroll_arrows_that_scroll_on_hover` (kittest): a 3-item menu shows no arrows, an 80-item menu in a 300 pt window overflows, and hovering the lower arrow scrolls it down.
