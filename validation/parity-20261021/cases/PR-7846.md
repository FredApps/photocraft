Fixes #7833

## Cause
The effect `noise()` step skipped only pixels with coverage of exactly 0. It added a fixed-size offset `(n - 0.5) * 2 * amount` to every other pixel. The Softer glow and shadow blur leave tiny non-zero values across their whole rectangular reach, so pixels the glow barely touches (around 1e-18) got up to about 0.5 coverage from noise. That made a speckled rectangle that the map bounds cut off.

## Fix
Noise now scales the coverage, `v * (1 + (n - 0.5) * 2 * amount)`, instead of adding a fixed offset. The speckle fades out with the glow or shadow and can no longer show up where the effect itself is invisible. Full noise can still punch holes in the solid part.
