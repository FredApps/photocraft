Fixes #5262.



From 4 px up, the Pencil cursor (and the Eraser's in Pencil mode) was a smooth circle inside the dab's whole-pixel square. An aliased dab paints a stair-stepped disc of whole pixels: 4 px fills 12 pixels, 5 px fills 21, 9 px fills 69. The circle cut through the edge pixels, so it didn't show which ones a click would change. It also ignored roundness and angle: a 9 px tip at 50 % roundness showed the circle over a 37-pixel ellipse, and a 2.6 px tip showed a 3×3 square over a 5-pixel plus. In a rotated view, the 1–3 px square and the Block-mode Eraser's square were drawn as an upright box between two of their turned corners.



Trace the dab with the renderer's own rasterizer (`outline::aliased_outline`, next to `tip_outline`) and draw the boundary of the pixels it fills, with the dark-outside, light-inside one-pixel lines the square used. The outline is traced once per brush change and cached like `brush_cursor`'s tip outline, then moved by whole pixels to the pointer's grid cell and mapped through the view, so it turns, flips and follows Pixel Aspect Ratio Correction. The Block-mode Eraser's square takes the same path, at every zoom.



The Pencil falls back to the tip's smooth outline from `brush_cursor` below 100 %, at 100 % when Pixel Aspect Ratio Correction makes a document pixel narrower than a physical one (a ratio below 1), for a tip too big to trace (round over 508 px, sampled over about 359 px), for a tip so ragged its outline has more than 16,384 corners, and for a tip that fills no pixel. Below 100 % that outline sits on the pointer, as an OS cursor bitmap where one can show it; the dab lands within half a physical pixel of the pointer there. From 100 % up it is painted where the dab lands, as the square's circle was; at 800 % the dab's centre can be 4 points either way from the pointer. Under 6 points across the cursor still adds the hotspot crosshair, on the pointer, as the old cursor did. Past the size cap the smooth outline is within a pixel of the stair-steps, and a tip over the cap is turned away before `BrushContext::new` builds a sampled tip's mip pyramid, which would only be thrown away.



Also:

- The line colours come from `Tokens::cursor_outline`, like the other brush cursors, so the light line's alpha is 235 instead of 230.

- Both outline caches, `pencil_cursor`'s and `brush_cursor`'s, now trace a stored tip again once the preset store loads its full bitmap. Stored tips compare equal whether loaded or not, so a cursor first drawn during loading kept the preview's outline until the brush changed.



This follows echelon's note on #2214: Photoshop paints a pixelated circle for a round Pencil tip (#1057), so the cursor should outline that circle.



Tests:

- `pencil_tests::the_pencil_cursor_outlines_the_pixels_its_dab_fills` drives the real canvas at 800 % and compares the pixel-grid edges the cursor strokes with the boundary of a real `paint.pencil` dab at the same point. Its 35 cases cover sizes 1–12 at two sub-pixel positions, 2.6 and 4.4 px, roundness 0.5 at 0° and 30°, a rotated and flipped view, and the Eraser in Pencil and Block mode. With the base `canvas.rs`, 28 of them fail.

- `a_real_click_paints_exactly_what_the_pencil_cursor_outlined` and two siblings click the canvas 62 times and check that each click changes exactly the pixels the cursor outlined just before, in one undo step. They cover an asymmetric sampled tip turned and flipped, a squashed tip on every quarter turn of the view, the Pencil- and Block-mode Eraser on an opaque layer, 2×, 1.5× and 1.25× displays, and Pixel Aspect Ratio Correction at 2 and 0.91. With the base `canvas.rs`, 34 of 37, 10 of 13 and (at 2×) 4 of 4 clicks fail.

- `outline::tests` (paint) compare the traced outline with one-point aliased strokes at 8, 16 and 32 bits and in every colour mode: a tip with a hole, a thin 45° tip whose pixels touch only at corners, Block squares, two asymmetric tips turned, squashed and flipped (1,512 dabs), a seeded fuzz of 600 brushes, 810 hostile but finite size, roundness and angle combinations, and the 508 px limit. NaN, infinite, negative and 5000 px sizes give one pixel or no outline, without a panic. `the_cap_is_checked_before_the_mipmaps_are_built` checks that the early cap check uses the box the context's `dab_rect` gives, for 1,458 brushes: round, sampled, unusable and stored tips (loaded or not), sizes around both limits and sizes the renderer bounds, with roundness, angle, flips and the Block square.

- `pencil_cursor::tests` check where the two lines land (on physical pixels at 1×, 1.5× and 2×, and either side of every corner on turned, mirrored and stretched views), the fallback below 100 % and its hotspot, the Block square from 1 % to 6,400 %, and the cache. `at_100_percent_the_outline_is_drawn_at_any_ui_scale` covers UI scales where the 100 % check fell just short, since `(1 / ppp) · ppp` is 0.99999994 in f32: ppp 0.85 (85 % on a 1× display), 1.7 (85 % on 2×) and 2.25 × 1.1. `from_100_percent_an_untraceable_tip_is_centred_on_its_dab` checks that a tip over the cap is drawn around the dab's centre for any pointer over the dab: round at 100 %, 300 % and 800 %, squashed and turned, and sampled.

- `a_stored_tip_is_traced_again_once_its_full_bitmap_loads` (in `pencil_cursor` and in `brush_cursor`), `below_100_percent_a_tiny_pencil_still_shows_its_hotspot`, `at_100_percent_the_outline_is_drawn_at_any_ui_scale` and `from_100_percent_an_untraceable_tip_is_centred_on_its_dab` fail without their fixes.



`square_cursor_sits_on_the_pixel_grid` and `the_cursor_is_round_exactly_when_the_pencil_dab_is` go with the helpers they tested (`pencil_cursor_rect`, `pencil_tip_is_round`).



### Validation



On 7adba7b4:

- `cargo test -p photocraft-paint --lib`: 113 passed.

- `cargo test -p photocraft-ui-egui --lib`: 2,939 passed, 13 ignored.

- `cargo clippy -p photocraft-paint -p photocraft-ui-egui --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo fmt -p photocraft-paint -p photocraft-ui-egui -- --check`: clean.

- `cargo xtask layers`: OK, 30 crates, no violations. `cargo xtask wasm`: OK.

- An offscreen wgpu render (throwaway test) of the 3, 4, 9 and 40 px cursors over a real dab at 300–1600 %, plus a 50 % roundness tip at 30° and a 30° rotated, flipped view: the outline sits on the dab's pixel edges in each.

- Tracing one dab in a release build, medians of five runs, ranges over five sessions on a machine running other builds: a round tip takes 0.09–0.16 ms at 100 px and 1.8–2.7 ms at 508 px; a sampled tip with a 64 × 64 bitmap 0.3–0.5 ms at 100 px and 4.0–6.0 ms at 359 px. A sampled tip with a 2500 × 2500 bitmap takes 11.7–15.1 ms at 100 px, nearly all of it the mip pyramid `BrushContext::new` builds (11.6–15.2 ms on its own), which each stroke with that tip builds too; nothing keeps it between strokes. Without the early cap check, the same tip took as long at 400 and 700 px (11.3–15.0 ms) to give no outline; with it, under a microsecond. With a 1024 px cap, a 1020 px round tip took 8.3 ms and a 721 px sampled tip 17.1 ms (one run), longer than a 60 Hz frame, so the cap is 512.



Notes:

- From 100 % up the outline stays snapped to the pixel grid, as the square was, so it shows what a click paints. #1057 also asks for Photoshop's unsnapped cursor; this doesn't change that.

- The cursor shows the plain tip at full pressure. Shape Dynamics, Scattering, Texture and the Dual Brush aren't reflected, and neither is Brush Pose › Override Pressure: with Pressure for Size on (the default), a mouse click then paints that fraction of the size, so a 20 px tip at 50 % fills 80 pixels, not the 316 outlined. Otherwise a mouse click is at full pressure, so Pressure for Size alone doesn't change it (a pen's lighter press does), and Transfer changes how much a click paints, not which pixels. The Ctrl+Alt resize preview still draws a smooth circle.

- `scorecard/tools.toml` TOOL-213-1 ("square pixel-aligned cursor") and the cursor row in `docs/brush-parity.md`, which #4420 also edits, aren't updated here.
