## Pinned upstream reproduction and regression specification

Refs storytold/PhotoCraft#5745. Split out of #5816 per review.

**Cause:** Auto-Blend's work area was the selected layers' union cropped to the canvas. Layers that Auto-Align leaves partly off the canvas got masks that hid everything past the canvas edge, so Reveal All showed a cut-off panorama. Photoshop masks the whole layer.

**Fix:** the area is now the whole union. If the union is more than twice the larger of the layers' total content and the canvas (layers far apart), it still keeps to the canvas. When every layer is inside the canvas, the area is the same as before, for stack mode too.

**Test:** `auto_blend_panorama_masks_past_the_canvas`: a layer hanging off the right edge shows at x=300 in its mask and in the merged layer.

Tested: `cargo test -p photocraft-engine --lib align_cmds::tests::auto_blend` (5 pass, the new test among them). Independent of #5816 and of the other split PRs; based on current main.
