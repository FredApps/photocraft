Fixes #7384

Follow-up to the review of #6670 (Content-Aware Fill with nothing to sample, #6532), which suggested it as a separate change.

### Problem
In the Content-Aware Fill workspace (#2376):
- **Black preview.** When the sampling area had no pixels outside the fill area (the Sampling Brush erased it all under Auto or Rectangular), the live preview showed the black fill such an area produces. It gave no hint why.
- **OK lost the work.** OK closed the workspace even when the fill failed. The error only reached the status bar, and the sampling strokes and fill edits were lost. With #6670, an empty sampling area is one such failure (the command refuses it); a locked layer is another, already today. The control channel's `commit` behaved the same way.

### Solution
`crates/ui-egui/src/caf_ui.rs`:
- **Preview.** `compute_preview` returns "nothing to sample from: the sampling area has no pixels outside the fill area" when the plan's `source` is empty, the same words the engine's refusal in #6670 uses. The panel already shows preview errors in place of the image, so the user sees what is wrong instead of a black fill. The check is in the preview, not in `content_aware_plan_with`; an empty Custom area with nothing painted yet already fails earlier, in the plan, with "custom sampling needs `area`…".
- **OK.** OK closes only when the fill ran. On an error the workspace stays open, and the error goes to the panel (`report`, next to the controls) as well as the status bar, so the areas can be fixed and OK pressed again. Apply's errors go through the same `report`.
- **Control channel.** `commit` keeps the workspace open on an error too, and still returns the error.
- **Undo / Redo.** Undoing or redoing a sampling stroke clears that panel error, as a new stroke does. The other steps already clear it through `rebase`. Otherwise the stale error would stay drawn over the recovered preview.

**Merge after #6670.** The preview check is local, and a failed OK keeps the workspace open whatever the error. But until #6670 makes the engine refuse an empty sampling area, OK would still fill black there while the preview already says "nothing to sample from". This should land after #6670; together they catch it in both places.

### Tests
`caf_ui::tests` (both fail before this change):
- `an_erased_sampling_area_previews_as_an_error`:
  - Rectangular, then a subtracting stroke wider than the window: 0 sampled pixels, and the preview error is "nothing to sample from";
  - undo brings the preview back.
- `a_failed_ok_keeps_the_workspace_open`:
  - with the layer's pixels locked, OK (the button's `commit`) leaves the workspace open, with the error in its panel and the status bar, and so does the control channel's `commit`;
  - unlocked, OK fills and closes.
- `undo_and_redo_of_a_stroke_clear_a_failed_oks_error`: after a failed OK, Undo of the sampling stroke clears the panel error; after another failed OK, Redo clears it too.
