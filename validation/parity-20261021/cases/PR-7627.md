Fixes #7233

### Problem
`filter.other.minimum` and `filter.other.maximum` take `preserve: "squareness" | "roundness"`, but their `.atn` event (`Mnm ` / `Mxm `) maps the radius only. A step with `preserve: "roundness"` was exported without the option, even by strict export. On import it replayed as the default Squareness, which changes the shape at the corners and therefore the pixels and any mask made from them, and nothing was reported.

### Solution
`crates/engine/src/atn_cmds.rs`:
- `ev!` gains an optional `unless` guard: params the descriptor can't carry, so the step isn't exported. That is the same treatment `pixel_target` already gives a step aimed at a mask or channel.
- Minimum and Maximum use it with `preserves_roundness`.

As a result, strict export refuses an action holding such a step and names it. Export with `skipUnsupported` leaves the step out and lists it as skipped. Squareness, explicit or by default, exports as before.

As the issue suggests, this refuses the case rather than guessing Photoshop's descriptor for the shape option. Without a Photoshop-recorded action using Roundness, the encoding can't be verified; once one is available, the shape can be mapped and this guard dropped.

### Tests
`atn_cmds::tests::minimum_and_maximum_with_roundness_are_not_exported_as_squareness`, for both filters (fails before this change):
- with `preserve: "roundness"`, strict export is an error, and lenient export reports the step as skipped;
- with no `preserve` or `"squareness"`, the step exports and imports back with radius 2 and no other shape.
