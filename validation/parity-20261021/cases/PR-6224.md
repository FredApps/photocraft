Fixes #6203.

The Free Transform warp samples in premultiplied alpha, but it clamped the accumulated alpha to 1 before un-premultiplying. Photoshop's Bicubic (Keys, a = -0.75) overshoots, so next to partly transparent pixels (the soft edge of a sprite, or a piece that was already rotated once) the alpha comes out a little above 1, and dividing by the clamped value scaled the colour up by the same amount: a light rim along the rotated edge, pure white on light textures as in the screenshots. Multiples of 90° land on the pixel grid and never overshoot, which is why they looked clean. The fix divides by the unclamped alpha and still stores the clamped one. The mesh warp (Edit > Transform > Warp) had the same line, so it gets the same change.
