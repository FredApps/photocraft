Fixes #4895

`grid_center` used `floor` for odd-sized tips while the line walker treats values within `1e-7` of a pixel boundary as exactly on it. A 1 px Pencil Shift-click just below a grid corner could therefore snap to one cell on pointer-down and rasterize into its neighbor on release.

Reuse the line walker's grid-cell rule for odd-sized tip snapping. This keeps the click endpoint, cursor, and rasterized line on the same pixel. Add unit and UI regression coverage, including the TL/TR/BL/BR/center subpixel matrix.

Related report: #8040.
