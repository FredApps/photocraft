## What and why

Under the Studio-layout themes, Window › Adjustments (and the rail's Properties button) showed the floating **Properties** card with the document's properties. The Adjustments grid could not be reached.

The menu side already works: `view_cmds::invoke` runs before the `panel_alias` fallback, and `panel_tab` maps `window.panel.adjustments` to `("properties", 1)`, so the command shows Properties and selects tab 1 (`window_panels_select_dock_tabs` covers this). The Pro dock draws tab 1 as `adjustments_grid` (`dock_body`). Studio doesn't dock Properties, though. `properties_window` floats it as a card that only ever called `properties_body` and ignored `dock_tabs.properties`.

- The card's title becomes the group's pill tab strip (`tab_strip::pill_tabs`, as Studio's docked cards use), **Properties | Adjustments**. Tab 1 draws `adjustments_grid`. The strip follows and sets `dock_tabs.properties`, so the menu, its checkmark and the Pro dock agree with the card. Picking an adjustment switches back to Properties, as in the dock.
- It uses `dock.visible_tabs`, so a tab closed in the dock stays closed on the card. Its context menu works too: Close hides one tab (and the card with the last), and Close Tab Group hides the card. That close logic moved from the dock's `Action::CloseTab` into `dock::close_tab`, so both use the same code.
- The title bar still drags the card. It is registered before the tabs (as in `widgets::card_ex`), so the tabs keep their clicks.
