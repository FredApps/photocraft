Fixes #6810.



With a dialog open, the canvas still pans under it (Hand tool, Space-drag, middle-drag). `dialogs::pan_delta` decided whether a drag is a canvas pan by checking that the press origin is outside every dialog rect. But when you drag a dialog by its title, the dialog follows the pointer, so after a few pixels the original press point is no longer inside the (moved) dialog. From then on the title-bar drag counted as a canvas drag and the image moved with the window.



Fix: `press_began_on_dialog` records, on the frame of the press, whether the press landed on a dialog, and keeps that answer until the next press. `pan_delta` ignores a held press that began on a dialog. `free_press` (canvas tools under a dialog: eyedroppers, Field Blur pins, Refine Edge brush, Layer Style shadow drag) uses the same check, so a dialog drag can't reach those tools either, e.g. when the pointer runs ahead of last frame's dialog rect during a fast drag.



Dragging the image itself on the free canvas (point 1 of the issue) works as before: a press that begins outside the dialogs still pans.



Test: `dragging_a_dialog_title_does_not_pan_the_canvas` presses on a dialog, then moves the dialog away from the press origin while the button is held, and checks that neither `pan_delta` nor `free_press` reports a canvas drag. Without the fix `pan_delta` returns the pointer movement.
