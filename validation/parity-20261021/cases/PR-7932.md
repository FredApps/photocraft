Fixes #7824

### Problem
⌘/Ctrl-click on a layer, mask or vector-mask thumbnail in the Layers panel loads it as a selection, and ⇧, ⌥ and ⇧⌥ make that add, subtract or intersect. Hovering the thumbnail with those keys held left the plain arrow, so nothing said what the click would do. Photoshop changes the pointer over the thumbnail and adds the selection mode's +, − or × badge.

### Solution
`crates/ui-egui/src/panels.rs`, Layers panel row:
- **One test for "would load":** the ⌘-click check (which thumbnail, and not the Background, which has no transparency) is now one closure, `load_at`. Both the click and the hover use it, so the cursor shows exactly where a click loads.
- **Cursor:** over such a thumbnail with ⌘ held, the pointer is a pointing hand.
- **Badge:** with ⇧, ⌥ or ⇧⌥ also held, the hand gets the badge the selection tools already draw for add, subtract and intersect (`tool_feedback::draw_badge`). ⌘ alone (a new selection) has no badge, as for the selection tools. The mode comes from `channels_panel::load_operation`, the same as the click.

The click itself is unchanged.

### Tests
`layer_shortcut_tests::command_over_a_thumbnail_shows_the_load_cursor`:
- the badge for ⌘, ⌘⇧, ⌘⌥ and ⌘⇧⌥ is none, add, subtract and intersect;
- in the running app (kittest), hovering a layer's thumbnail with ⌘, ⌘⇧ or ⌘⌥ shows the pointing hand;
- without ⌘, or over the Background's thumbnail, it doesn't.

It fails without the change: the pointer over the thumbnail stays the arrow.
