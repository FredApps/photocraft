## Eyedropper: a large sampling ring that reads on any colour (#6973)

The comparison ring drawn while the Eyedropper is held was 22 pt across with 4 pt arcs, outlined only by a thin semi-transparent dark stroke. Over a flat area of the colour being sampled the upper arc had the image's own colour and disappeared, so the ring looked broken (https://github.com/storytold/photocraft/issues/6973).

Now it follows Photoshop's structure, measured from the screenshots in the issue (316 px vs the old 42 px on the same screen):

- an **opaque neutral grey outer band** (~17 pt) around the pointer,
- a **coloured band of the same width** inside it: the sampled colour on the upper half, the current foreground on the lower, meeting at 3 and 9 o'clock as in Photoshop (the old gap is gone),
- **thin dark and light hairlines** (one physical pixel) on the ring's two edges, so every boundary reads even on a flat mid-grey that matches the band itself,
- the **pipette stays visible at the centre**, its tip on the sampled pixel — a ring this size says nothing about where it samples without it.

Sizes are in points, so the ring scales with Interface › UI Scale. `Show Sampling Ring` off and Preferences › Cursors › Precise keep their old fallbacks.

### Implementation notes

- `eyedropper_ring` in `crates/ui-egui/src/canvas.rs` draws the bands with `circle_stroke` (which in egui 0.36 paints its stroke *outside* the radius) and the upper half-ring with a centred `Shape::line` arc.
- New integration test `crates/ui-egui/tests/eyedropper_ring.rs` renders the real app offscreen over a flat `#7f7f7f` document — exactly the ring grey, the worst case — and asserts both ring edges and the pipette differ from the background, and that nothing is drawn with Show Sampling Ring off. Verified visually with the offscreen renders.
