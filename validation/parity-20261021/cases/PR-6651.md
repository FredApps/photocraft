## What
The Patterns panel only had small procedurally generated tiles. This adds a curated
library of 38 photographic pattern tiles built from CC0 ambientCG material scans,
grouped like Photoshop's default folders: Brick & Tile, Concrete, Fabric, Foliage,
Ground, Marble, Metal, Paper, Plants, Wood.

Each tile is the material's Color (albedo) map, downscaled to 512x512 and embedded
from crates/engine/assets/patterns/. The set is generated once and shared (a session
clones the patterns, whose tiles are Arc).

## Licences and clean-room
ambientCG releases its scans into the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal, so the
tiles are free to embed and redistribute. No Photoshop or other proprietary asset was
used. Per-file sources are in crates/engine/assets/patterns/SOURCES.md, the licence
ships as LICENSE-cc0.txt, and ATTRIBUTION.md and NOTICE carry the rows.

## Size
The 38 tiles are about 3.8 MB (JPEG q92). They are native-only: on the web that would
push the wasm past its per-file cap, so photographic() is empty under wasm32 and the
procedural tiles are identical there.
