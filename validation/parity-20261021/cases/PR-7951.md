Fixes #7940

### Problem
With the Note tool, clicking a note selected it (and showed its text in the Notes panel), but the options bar's Author field and colour chip kept showing the tool's own values. In a PSD with notes by several people, such as ag-psd's `annotations` sample, the bar didn't say whose note or which colour was selected. Photoshop shows the selected note's author and colour there.

### Solution
`crates/ui-egui/src/analysis_ui.rs`, new `show_note_options`:
- **Canvas:** a Note tool click on a note copies that note's author and colour into the options bar.
- **Notes panel:** the same happens when the panel's Previous and Next buttons change the selected note. Opening the panel alone doesn't change the bar.
- **New notes:** a click off the notes adds one with what the bar shows, so it follows the last note picked, as in Photoshop. Typing another author or picking another colour still changes it.

### Tests
`analysis_ui::tests::clicking_a_note_shows_its_author_and_color`:
- with two notes ("Ann" pink and "Bob" blue) and the bar set to "Me" yellow, clicking each note shows its own author and colour;
- a click off the notes then adds a note by "Bob";
- the notes themselves are unchanged.

It fails without the change: the bar keeps "Me".
